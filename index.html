Grammar Focus

HAVE TO

We use "Have to" to talk about External Obligations. Rules, laws, or physical facts that we cannot control.

The Rule Book

  • 🚀 "I have to wear a spacesuit outside." (Fact: Or I will die)
  • 💻 "The computer says we have to fix the engine." (External instruction)
  • ❌ Negative: "He doesn't have to work today." (It is not necessary)

Grammar Focus

MUST

We use "Must" to talk about Internal Obligations. Personal duties, strong feelings, or urgent commands.

The Heart & Urgency

  • 🌍 "I must save the Earth." (Personal duty / My heart says so)
  • ⚠️ "You must wake up now!" (Urgent command)
  • ❌ Negative: "We mustn't fail." (Prohibition / Strictly forbidden)

The Difference

Must vs Have To

HAVE TO

The universe forces you.

"I have to eat food every day."

MUST

Your morality forces you.

"I must help my friend. He is in danger."
